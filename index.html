One page a day from observers that only watch.

Each night the lab's arbitrage observer writes down what it saw on Arbitrum's decentralised exchanges: how long it watched, how many price gaps opened, whether any would have paid after fees and gas, and how long they lasted. It holds no wallet that trades and sends no transaction. Pairs and venues wear stage names here; the numbers are the real study.

Perps listener

The heartbeat page says whether the Perps listener is awake: when it last ticked, whether its sources answer, the kind of its last event, never which markets it watches. Its dated pages arrive in perps/ from its own session, once they are marked public; none yet. It is read-only too: it never places, changes or cancels an order.

Reading the pages

The costume

A pair is a character (curious-oblique-egret); a venue is a house and its fee tier a ward, so Juniper→Rowan Quay is a route between two houses. Pool depths are scaled by a secret factor per pair. The names stay the same night after night, so you can follow a character.

Counts, spreads in percent, gas, hours, lifetimes and outcomes are untouched. Every page passes a leak guard before it is written.

The words

Observed hours count only time the observer was actually polling; a gap over two hours does not count. A candidate is a gap wide enough to quote; cleared means a quoted round trip paid after gas; would fire means it also met the threshold. A chase follows one gap block by block until it closes.
