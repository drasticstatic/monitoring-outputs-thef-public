Is the Perps listener awake?

The listener keeps watch between the desk's check-ins and wakes one only when something changes. It is read-only: it never places, changes or cancels an order. Each tick it rewrites a small heartbeat; this page reads it and shows only its times and counts, never which markets it watches.

Reading it

The three words

Awake: the last tick is within two cadences and no source is silent. Listening, a source is silent: it ticks, but a source has not answered for ten minutes (the desk gets a phone note, and another when it answers). Stale: no tick for longer than two cadences; it is stopped, or its newest ticks have not been published yet.

What this page leaves out

Which markets are watched, their prices, and which market an event was about. Only the kind of the last event shows (a zone touch, a funding flip, a stall). Nothing on this page is invented: with no heartbeat it says so.
